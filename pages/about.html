✦ ABOUT THE PROJECT ✦

Meet Prathibimba AI

A Dasara-inspired AI experience that turns facial expressions into playful Kannada-English reactions.

🧠

What is Prathibimba?

Prathibimba means reflection. Our project explores how artificial intelligence can observe facial expressions and turn them into an interactive experience.

Instead of simply showing an AI result, Prathibimba makes the interaction fun, fast and engaging for everyone.

🪔

Dasara Inspiration

The project combines the festive spirit of Dasara with modern artificial intelligence.

💻

Technology

Built using web technologies, camera-based face analysis and AI-powered expression detection.
